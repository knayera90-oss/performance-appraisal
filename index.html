AI-Powered Performance Management System

Complete all 10 sections, then click "Calculate Results" to see the final score and submit to HR.

How it works: Weighted scores are computed across 6 categories (2026 Goals 40% · Competencies 25% · Quality 10% · Attendance 10% · Violations 5% · Development 10%). Attendance and Violations are scored automatically in the background. If no training was provided this year, the Development category is excluded so the employee is not penalized. After submission, the detailed breakdown, AI insights and recommendations are sent to HR only — staff see the final total score only.

1. Employee Information

Type your full name below — this acts as your electronic signature.

2. Evaluating 2026 Goals

Weight: 40%

Enter up to 6 goals for 2026. Goals 1–3 are mandatory. For each goal, enter the achieved percentage (0-100%). The system calculates the achievement score automatically.

3. Core Competencies

Weight: 25%

Rate every competency 1–5 based on observed behavior during the review period. All competencies are mandatory.

4. Quality of Work

Weight: 10%

Rate every quality item 1–5. All items are mandatory.

5. Attendance

Weight: 10%

Rate the employee's overall attendance commitment 1–10 (10 = perfect, 1 = very poor). The system converts this to a score automatically — you do not choose the final rating.

6. Violations & Penalties

Weight: 5%

Number of violations or penalties submitted to HR during the review period (if none, enter 0). Penalties apply automatically: 1–2 violations = −0.5, 3–5 = −1, 6–10 = −2, 10+ = score drops to 1. HR will review and verify this count for accuracy.

7. 2027 Objectives

The employee lists their objectives for 2027 — at least one objective is mandatory.

8. Development & Training

Weight: 10%

If the employee was not offered any training this year, select "No" — the Development category will be excluded from the score so they are not penalized for something outside their control.

Mandatory for the direct manager to complete — regardless of whether training was received or not.
