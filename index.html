AI-Powered Performance Management System

Complete all 8 sections, then click "Calculate Results" to see the final score and submit to HR.

How it works: Weighted scores are computed across the evaluation categories (2026 Goals 40% · Competencies 25% · Quality 15% · Attendance 10% · Development 10%). Attendance is scored automatically in the background from the reported incident count — no rating is chosen on the form. If no training was provided this year, the Development category is excluded so the employee is not penalized. After submission, the detailed breakdown, AI insights and recommendations are sent to HR only — staff see the final total score only.

1. Employee Information

Select the review period covered by this appraisal.

Type your full name below — this acts as your electronic signature.

2. Direct Manager

The direct manager types their full name below — this acts as their electronic signature.

3. Evaluating 2026 Goals

Weight: 40%

Enter up to 6 goals for 2026. Goals 1–3 are mandatory. For each goal, enter its weight % (all weights should add up to 100%) and rate achievement 1–5: 1 = Not achieved, 3 = Partially achieved, 5 = Fully exceeded.

4. Core Competencies

Weight: 25%

Rate every competency 1–5 based on observed behavior during the review period. All competencies are mandatory.

5. Quality of Work

Weight: 15%

Rate every quality item 1–5. All items are mandatory.

6. Attendance Discipline

Weight: 10%

Enter the number of attendance discipline incidents during the review period (e.g. late arrivals, unreported absences). The attendance score is calculated automatically by the system — you do not choose a rating. HR will review and verify this count for accuracy and may adjust it after submission.

7. Development & Training

Weight: 10%

If the employee was not offered any training this year, select "No" — the Development category will be excluded from the score so they are not penalized for something outside their control.

Mandatory for the direct manager to complete — regardless of whether training was received or not.

8. Direct Manager Comments

Describe what this employee does exceptionally well.

Describe where this employee should focus their development.

Write your overall assessment of the employee's performance this period.
